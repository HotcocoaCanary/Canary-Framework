# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者公约行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺，作为社区成员、贡献者和负责人，让参与本项目成为对所有人都无骚扰的体验，无论其
年龄、体型、残疾（可见或不可见）、民族、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、
社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、种姓、肤色、宗教或性取向如何。

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

我们承诺以有助于建立开放、友好、多元、包容、健康社区的方式行事和互动。

## Our Standards / 我们的标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括：

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people / 对他人表现出同理心与善意
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences / 尊重不同的意见、观点与经验
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback / 给出并优雅地接受建设性反馈
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
  from the experience / 为自己的错误负责并向受影响者致歉，并从中学习
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community / 关注对整体社区最有利的事

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

不可接受的行为包括：

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind /
  使用性化语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性挑逗
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks /
  挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- Public or private harassment / 公开或私下骚扰
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission / 未经明确许可发布他人私人信息
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting /
  在专业场合可被合理视为不当的其他行为

## Enforcement Responsibilities / 执行责任

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable
behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

社区负责人有责任澄清并执行可接受行为的标准，并针对任何被视为不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为
采取适当、公正的纠正措施。

## Scope / 范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the community in public spaces.

本行为准则适用于所有社区空间，也适用于个人在公共场合正式代表本社区的情形。

## Enforcement / 执行

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
community leaders responsible for enforcement at **Cacao.Canary@outlook.com**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

如有滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，请通过 **Cacao.Canary@outlook.com** 向负责执行的社区
负责人举报。所有投诉都将被及时、公正地审查与调查。

## Enforcement Guidelines / 执行指南

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences
for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

社区负责人将遵循以下社区影响指南，决定对任何违反本行为准则的行为采取何种后果：

### 1. Correction / 纠正

**Community Impact / 社区影响**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community. 使用不当语言，或其他被视为不专业或不受欢迎的行为。

**Consequence / 后果**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around
the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public
apology may be requested. 由社区负责人发出一次私下书面警告，说明违规性质及不当原因；可要求公开道歉。

### 2. Warning / 警告

**Community Impact / 社区影响**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.
通过单一事件或一系列行为造成的违规。

**Consequence / 后果**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the
people involved for a specified period of time. 一次警告，并说明持续违规的后果；在指定时间内不得与
相关人员互动。

### 3. Temporary Ban / 临时封禁

**Community Impact / 社区影响**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behavior. 严重违反社区标准，包括持续不当行为。

**Consequence / 后果**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with
the community for a specified period of time. 在指定时间内，禁止与社区进行任何形式的互动或公开交流。

### 4. Permanent Ban / 永久封禁

**Community Impact / 社区影响**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards,
including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or
disparagement of classes of individuals. 表现出持续违反社区标准的行为模式，包括持续不当行为、骚扰
个人、或对某类群体的攻击或贬损。

**Consequence / 后果**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
永久禁止在社区内进行任何形式的公开互动。
